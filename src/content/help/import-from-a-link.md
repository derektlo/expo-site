Spotted a recipe on a food blog or website? Expo can pull the full recipe out of almost any link, so you don't have to copy and paste ingredients and steps by hand.

## How to import from a link

1. Copy the web address of the recipe you want.
2. In Expo, start an import and paste the link.
3. Expo reads the page and extracts the recipe — title, ingredients, and steps.
4. Review the result, make any edits you like, and save it to your recipes.

## What gets imported

Expo pulls out the parts of a recipe that matter:

- The recipe title
- The ingredient list with quantities
- The step-by-step instructions

Once imported, the recipe behaves like any other recipe in Expo: you can cook it, personalize it, add it to a meal plan, or share it.

## If an import doesn't look right

Some pages are formatted in unusual ways. If a detail is missing or off, you can edit the recipe after importing to fix it. If a particular site never imports cleanly, let us know at support@heyexpo.com so we can take a look.
