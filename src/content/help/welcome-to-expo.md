Expo is an AI-native recipe app. It helps you collect recipes from anywhere, generate brand-new ones tailored to your taste, cook them hands-free, plan your week, and share the food you love with friends and family.

## What you can do with Expo

- **[Import recipes from anywhere](/help/import-from-a-link)** — paste a link, share a social post or video, or snap a photo of a cookbook page, and Expo pulls out the full recipe.
- **[Generate recipes with AI](/help/generate-a-recipe)** — describe what you're craving and Expo creates a complete recipe, personalized to your taste over time.
- **[Cook hands-free](/help/cook-mode-and-timers)** — follow step-by-step cooking mode, set timers, and ask questions out loud with the ["Hey Expo" voice assistant](/help/hey-expo-voice).
- **[Plan your week](/help/meal-plans)** — add recipes to a meal plan and turn it into a single shopping list.
- **[Share with people you cook with](/help/friends-and-sharing)** — build shared cookbooks, follow friends, and see what everyone is making.

## Getting started

1. Download Expo from the App Store and [create an account](/help/create-an-account).
2. Answer a few quick taste questions during onboarding so recommendations feel right from day one.
3. Import a recipe you already love, or generate your first one with AI.
4. When you're ready to cook, open the recipe and tap into cooking mode.

## Where to find help

You can browse this help center any time, or tap the chat bubble to ask the support assistant a question. You can also reach the team directly at support@heyexpo.com.
