You manage everything about your account from the **Settings** screen.

## Personal details

Go to **Settings → Personal Details** to view and update your account information.

## Privacy

Under **Settings → Privacy**, the **Private Profile** toggle controls who can see your activity. When it's on, people must send a follow request you approve. When it's off, anyone can follow you. See [Friends, following, and shared cookbooks](/help/friends-and-sharing) for how following works.

## Notifications

Open **Settings → Notifications** to choose what Expo notifies you about. [Cooking timers](/help/cook-mode-and-timers) can send time-sensitive alerts so you don't miss them while a dish is on the stove.

## Premium

Expo offers a Premium subscription that unlocks additional capabilities. You can upgrade from **Settings** (look for **Upgrade to Premium**). Subscriptions are handled through your App Store account, so you manage billing, renewals, and cancellation in your Apple ID subscription settings.

## Sign out

To sign out, go to **Settings → Logout**. You'll need your email and password (or Face ID, if enabled) to sign back in.

## Delete your account

If you'd like to delete your account and your data, email support@heyexpo.com and the team will take care of it for you.

## Get help

For anything not covered here, tap the chat bubble to ask the support assistant, or email support@heyexpo.com.
