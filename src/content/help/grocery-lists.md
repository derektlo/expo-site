Expo turns the recipes you plan to cook into a smart shopping list, so you only buy what you actually need.

## Create a list from recipes

You can build a grocery list from a single recipe or from a whole meal plan. Expo gathers the ingredients and adds them to your list.

## Ingredients are merged

When the same ingredient appears in more than one recipe, Expo combines it into a single line on your list instead of repeating it. That means no duplicate entries and a clearer picture of how much to buy.

## Organize by aisle

Expo can group your list by store section — produce, dairy, pantry, and so on — so your list follows the path you actually walk through the store. This makes shopping faster and helps you avoid backtracking.

## Check things off

As you shop, check off items you've grabbed. Checked items move out of your way so you can focus on what's left.

## Sharing

If you share a meal plan with someone, the recipes in it are visible to them too, which makes it easy to coordinate who's cooking and shopping.
