A lot of the best recipes live in short videos and social posts. Expo can turn those into a proper recipe you can actually cook from.

## Import from a social post or video

1. In the app you're browsing (Instagram, TikTok, or YouTube), copy the link to the post or video — or use the share option.
2. Bring that link into Expo and start an import.
3. Expo analyzes the post or video and reconstructs the recipe as ingredients and steps.
4. Review it, tweak anything you want, and save.

## What works best

- **Instagram and TikTok posts** that show a recipe being made or describe it in the caption.
- **YouTube cooking videos** where the steps are demonstrated or narrated.

Because social recipes are often loose or sped-up, Expo fills in the structure for you. Always give the result a quick read before cooking, and adjust quantities to taste.

## If something is missing

Short videos don't always spell out every measurement. If a quantity or step is vague, edit the recipe after importing to match what you intended. For recipes that consistently fail to import, email support@heyexpo.com with the link.
