Expo is better with the people you cook with. You can follow friends and family, share recipes, and build cookbooks together.

## Follow friends

Search for people you know and follow them to see what they're cooking. Following someone adds their activity to your [feed](/help/activity-ratings-leaderboards).

## Private profiles and follow requests

If your profile is private, people have to send a follow request that you approve or decline, so you control who sees your activity. To make your profile private:

1. Go to **Settings**.
2. Under **Privacy**, turn on **Private Profile**.

When your profile is public, anyone can follow you and see your shared activity.

## Share a recipe

You can send a single recipe to someone, or share an entire collection. When a friend receives a shared recipe, they can save it into their own recipes and cook it themselves.

## Shared cookbooks

Build shared cookbooks with family or friends so everyone contributes to and cooks from the same set of recipes. It's a great way to keep family recipes in one place that everyone can reach.

## What others can see

People can see the activity and recipes you choose to share. Setting your profile to private limits this to followers you approve.
