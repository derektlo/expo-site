Expo can create a brand-new recipe from a description. Tell it what you're in the mood for and it writes a complete recipe — ingredients, steps, and timing.

## How to generate a recipe

1. Start a new recipe and choose to create one with AI.
2. Describe what you want. You can be as loose or specific as you like, for example:
   - "A cozy weeknight pasta with mushrooms"
   - "Something spicy with the chicken and peppers I have"
   - "A light, high-protein lunch for two"
3. Expo generates a full recipe. Review it, and if it's not quite right, revise it (see Personalize a recipe).
4. Save it to your recipes when you're happy.

## Make it from what you have

If you tell Expo which ingredients you have on hand, it will build the recipe around them and suggest sensible additions. This is a great way to use up what's in the fridge.

## It gets better over time

The more you use Expo — saving recipes, rating cooks, and giving feedback — the more your generated recipes reflect your personal taste. See **Your taste profile and match score** to learn how that works.

## Personalized to you

When Expo knows your taste, generation automatically takes it into account. You don't have to do anything special; it happens in the background.
