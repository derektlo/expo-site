Expo learns your culinary preferences over time and uses them to personalize recommendations and [recipe generation](/help/generate-a-recipe).

## How Expo learns your taste

Your taste profile is built from the signals you give as you use the app:

- Recipes you save and cook
- Ratings you leave after cooking
- Reactions you give to recipe suggestions (love, maybe, or not for me)
- The dietary preferences you set
- What you search for and ask Expo to make

You don't manage this directly — it updates quietly in the background as you use Expo. The more you cook and react, the sharper it gets.

## What the match score means

Some recipes show a **match percentage**. That's Expo's estimate of how well a recipe fits your taste, based on your profile. A higher percentage means it's more likely to be your kind of dish.

Match scores are a guide, not a rule. A lower score doesn't mean you won't enjoy something — it's just less aligned with your usual preferences. Trying recipes outside your usual range actually helps Expo learn.

## Dietary preferences

If you set dietary restrictions (for example, vegetarian or no dairy), Expo respects them when recommending and generating recipes. You can update these as your needs change.

## Cold start

When you're brand new, Expo doesn't know much about you yet, so recommendations start broad. They become more personal quickly as you cook and react to a few recipes.
