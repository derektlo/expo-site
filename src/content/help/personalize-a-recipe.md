Any recipe in Expo can be adapted to suit you. Whether it's one you generated, imported, or saved from a friend, you can revise it with a quick instruction.

## Revise a recipe

1. Open the recipe and choose to revise or edit it.
2. Describe the change you want in plain language, for example:
   - "Make it dairy-free"
   - "Swap the beef for mushrooms"
   - "Cut it down to two servings"
   - "Add more vegetables"
3. Expo rewrites the recipe with your change applied, keeping everything else intact.

## Quick edits

When you go to revise a recipe, Expo suggests a few quick-edit options tailored to that dish and your taste — like making it dairy-free or adding more veggies. Tap one to apply it instantly instead of typing.

## Scale the servings

Need to cook for more or fewer people? Ask Expo to scale the recipe (for example, "make it for six") and it adjusts the ingredient quantities for you.

## Smart substitutions

If you're missing an ingredient, ask for a substitution. Expo suggests a swap that fits the dish and keeps the result balanced.

Revisions create an updated version of the recipe you can save, so you always keep the version that works for you.
