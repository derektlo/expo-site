Most problems have a quick fix. Here are the ones we hear about most.

## An import didn't come out right

Some pages, posts, and photos are formatted in unusual ways. After importing, you can edit the recipe to fix any missing or incorrect details. See [Import a recipe from a link](/help/import-from-a-link), [from social and video](/help/import-from-social-and-video), or [from photos](/help/import-from-photos). If a specific source never imports cleanly, email support@heyexpo.com with the link or photo so we can improve it.

## A recipe doesn't match my taste

Expo gets to know your taste as you use it. Cook a few recipes, leave ratings, and react to suggestions (love, maybe, or not for me). Recommendations sharpen quickly once Expo has a little signal from you. See [Your taste profile and match score](/help/taste-profile-and-match).

## "Hey Expo" isn't responding

Make sure you've turned on **"Hey Expo" on Home** in **Settings → Voice**, and that you're on the Home tab — that's the only place the wake word listens. In a noisy kitchen, try saying it again a bit more clearly. You can always tap through a recipe by hand instead. For setup details, see [The "Hey Expo" voice assistant](/help/hey-expo-voice).

## I'm not getting timer alerts

Check that notifications are enabled for Expo in **Settings → Notifications** and in your device's system settings. [Cooking timers](/help/cook-mode-and-timers) use time-sensitive notifications so they can reach you while you're cooking.

## I can't sign in

Use the password reset option on the sign-in screen. If you've turned on Face ID or Touch ID and it isn't working, you can still sign in with your email and password. See [Creating an account and signing in](/help/create-an-account). Still stuck? Email support@heyexpo.com.

## Something else

Tap the chat bubble to ask the support assistant, or reach the team any time at support@heyexpo.com.
